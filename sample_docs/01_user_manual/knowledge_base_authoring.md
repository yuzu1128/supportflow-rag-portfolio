# Knowledge Base Authoring Guide

Source ID: manual-kb-authoring
Audience: Knowledge owners, support leads

## Article Structure

Each article should include a clear title, supported product area, last reviewed date, symptoms, resolution steps, and escalation notes. Short task articles retrieve better than long mixed-topic articles.

## Metadata

Required metadata:

- Product area
- Customer-facing visibility
- Owner team
- Review date
- Applies-to version
- Related error codes

## Draft and Review

Draft articles are visible only to agents. Published articles can appear in customer self-service search if visibility is customer-facing. Articles with API examples require engineering review before publishing.

## Retired Articles

Retired articles are excluded from suggestions, but remain searchable by administrators for audit purposes. Do not delete retired articles unless the retention period has expired.

## Style Rules

Use direct steps. Put warnings before destructive account or configuration actions. Avoid naming internal systems unless the customer needs the name to complete the task.
