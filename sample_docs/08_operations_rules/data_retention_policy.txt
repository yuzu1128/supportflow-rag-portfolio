Source ID: ops-data-retention
Title: SupportFlow Data Retention Policy

Conversation records:
Retain active and resolved conversation metadata for 24 months by default.

Internal notes:
Retain internal notes for 18 months unless a customer contract requires a shorter period.

Audit logs:
Retain role changes, API key changes, queue configuration changes, and export events for 24 months.

Knowledge articles:
Published and retired articles are retained while referenced by active support workflows. Retired articles can be removed after the retention review confirms no active dependency.

Redaction:
Exports redact token-like strings, email addresses, and phone-like strings by default. Administrators can request stricter export profiles.
