Source ID: incident-search-index-lag
Title: Search Index Lag Runbook

Trigger:
Open a SEV2 investigation when the median article indexing delay is above 15 minutes for 20 minutes, or when more than three customers report missing recent articles in agent suggestions.

Initial checks:
- Confirm document ingestion jobs are completing.
- Check vector index write latency.
- Compare keyword search and semantic search results for the same article.
- Verify that retired articles are not being returned as fresh results.

Mitigation:
- Disable large optional reindex batches.
- Prioritize new and recently updated support articles.
- Ask support leads to use direct article links if suggestions are stale.

Customer-facing wording:
"Article suggestions may be delayed for recently updated knowledge content. Existing published articles remain available through direct search."

Resolution target:
Backlog age below 5 minutes and manual spot checks passing for 10 recently updated articles.
