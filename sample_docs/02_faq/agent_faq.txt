Source ID: faq-agent
Title: Agent FAQ

Q: What should I do when an inquiry belongs to another queue?
A: Use Transfer, choose the destination queue, add a short internal note, and keep the priority unchanged unless the receiving queue has different SLA rules.

Q: When should I use Waiting on customer?
A: Use it after asking a customer for logs, screenshots, request IDs, or configuration details. The SLA response timer pauses only if the queue policy allows customer-wait pauses.

Q: Can I merge duplicate conversations?
A: Yes. Merge only when the same customer is asking about the same issue. The oldest conversation keeps the canonical conversation ID.

Q: What if a suggested article is wrong?
A: Mark the suggestion as not helpful and add a note with the correct article or missing topic. This feedback appears in the knowledge review queue.

Q: Can I close a reopened inquiry immediately?
A: Only if the new message confirms resolution or is unrelated. Otherwise keep the conversation open and respond based on the new context.
